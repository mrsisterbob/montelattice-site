What It Tracks

Regime

Dealer gamma

Deribit dealer gamma exposure. Its sign flips tend to precede a shift from range to trend.

Positioning

Funding & OI

Binance funding rates and open interest across BTC, ETH, and tracked alts, with BTC-dominance volatility.

Trigger

Breakout signals

A Donchian / ATR / RVOL breakout rule with a backtested edge, drawdown circuit breaker, and 2-stage risk.

Signal Format

What each emitted signal carries
FieldWhat it holds
SymbolThe trading pair the signal fired on
ConfidenceA score out of 100 from context, breakout and volume
ContextThe leading reason the rule fired, in one line
TriggeredWhen the signal fired

No sample figures here. Paper-trading only, no live capital. The live portfolio and signal feed run in the Console.

What I Learned

People say trading is something you can automate away. After building this, I found the code was not the hard part. The data input is, because good market data is either very expensive or very hard to get your hands on, and that is the part people selling trading bots tend to leave out.
