What I Learned

People say document filing is something you can automate away. After building DocFiler, I found the filing was not the hard part. Document tracking at a small firm carries a lot of compliance requirements that are very difficult to get around, so the audit below starts as a dry run on a sample of your files, before anything is touched.

How Working With Me Goes

  1. A free dry-run audit. I run DocFiler against a sample of your files and show you exactly what it would rename and where. Nothing is touched.
  2. Set up around your firm. It is configured against your own client list and document types, so it only files to clients you actually have.
  3. You approve the plan. Files move only after you have seen the report and said yes.
  4. Filing with a record. Documents are renamed and routed automatically, and every move is logged.
  5. A person for the unsure ones. Anything low-confidence is set aside for someone on your team to review.

Questions
