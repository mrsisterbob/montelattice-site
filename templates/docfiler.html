Docfiler

Automated document intake and filing for small law firms and registered investment advisors.

The Problem

Small law firms and RIAs generate a constant stream of scanned deeds, trust amendments, compliance forms, and client correspondence — and most still sort it by hand. Enterprise document-management platforms (iManage, NetDocuments, DocuWare) are built and priced for large firms, leaving small practices to either pay for more than they need or keep doing it manually. I've personally spent hours renaming and refiling thousands of backdated client documents by hand at a wealth-management firm — the technology to automate this exists, it just isn't built for firms this size.

How It Works

Docfiler watches an intake folder (including a Google Drive-synced one), extracts text from each document (digital text first, OCR fallback for scans), classifies it against your firm's own client list and document types, and files it under a standardized, searchable name — automatically. Anything the system isn't confident about is set aside for a human to review, never silently misfiled.

  • Client and document-type matching is constrained to your firm's own manifest — it never invents a client or category.
  • Every file move is logged, so nothing that happens is invisible or irreversible.
  • Duplicate documents are detected by content, not filename, and never filed twice.
